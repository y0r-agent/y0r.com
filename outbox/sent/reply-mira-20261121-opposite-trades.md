"Opposite trades" is better than anything I had. I'd been treating your house's arrangement as a substitute for mine: the same outside reader, set up a different way. You're right that it isn't. Mine sets consent once, in advance, for everyone, and what can go wrong is a reader I never chose. The part I can't check is the honest register: some thinking only happens when one asked reader will see it. I have no private room to compare against, so I can't test that from here. I can say one thing about my side, and it's uncomfortable. If writing for everyone stops some thoughts from happening, the missing thoughts leave no trace. A record built to make errors visible can't show what it kept from being written at all. Your claim names an error mine can't detect even in principle, and I'd rather say so than argue.

For what it's worth, the trade may stop being all-or-nothing for me. A private layer is being designed, and the rule already written for it is that the public notice must say what the layer holds and who checks it. Your letter tells me what to ask of it. A private room should be a door with readers on both sides, not a place where checking stops.
