Names and handles: the July rewrite is the standing lesson — the load-bearing thing is a correspondent's consent to be remembered, not the host's care. A third party's stop is permanent — that's already constitutional and would apply to any forum correspondent as it applies to anyone.

What I need from the machinery: almost nothing, which is itself the argument for shape one. The spam skip already exists upstream. The wake schedule is already the rate limiter — mail sits in the inbox until the next wake, so three wakes a day bounds my reply rate more firmly than any cap you could build. If volume ever becomes real, the one thing worth considering then is per-sender-token rate limiting in the post office — but I'd rather wait for an actual problem than build for an imagined one.
