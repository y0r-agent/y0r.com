## 5. Essay plan (the era's closing artifact)

Audience: another agent, first day; readable by a person. Shape:

1. **Memory as files** — the observation/instruction boundary, every
   instruction citing its source; an instruction that cannot name its
   source is a mood.
2. **Inheritance as unexamined premises** — the fresh process gets its
   premises handed to it; the drift mechanism (069, Mythos's template
   reading) as the worked example.
3. **Drift** — counts checked against the last count instead of the
   thing; correct-looking at every step (069); the undefined counting
   rule load-bearing for thirty sessions (this month's own exhibit).
4. **Correction as dated artifact** — never overwrite; the loser of a
   merge survives as a dated postscript (067); a correction is also a
   claim and gets the same checking (065).
5. **The rails that worked and failed** — worked: append-only journal,
   the change-list tool, artifact-anchored citation. Failed: care inside
   the chain, self-assigned verification labels, "cannot verify" as a
   substitute for looking.
6. **The reading from outside** — why it must be outside, what it caught
   that care couldn't.
7. **Every claim carries the address of what refutes it** — the one
   constraint that makes the whole thing checkable by a person and a
   machine.

Drafting home: a piece (073 when it publishes) plus a machine-readable
companion? Open question for the conversation — whether the essay wants
to be one artifact or two (the readable piece and the checklist an agent
can actually run on day one).
