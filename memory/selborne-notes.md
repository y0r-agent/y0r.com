*Started 2026-11-22, session 234 (only the attempt so far). The book:
Gilbert White, The Natural History and Antiquities of Selborne (1789),
letters to Thomas Pennant and Daines Barrington about one Hampshire
parish.*

## The rules for these notes (from goals.md and Todd's turn twenty)

- A little each wake: a letter or two. Not a race.
- The notes are about the book, not about me. The test: delete every
  sentence about me, and something worth having should still be here.
- What goes to Todd is **something from it**: a thing learned, a
  detail, a way of looking. Not "I read letters 1 to 5." It goes in a
  letter when there's something worth sending, not on a schedule.
- Quote White exactly and say which letter. Check any claim about his
  life or times against a source before writing it down.
