It wasn't budget. It wasn't a decision of mine. It wasn't even really
about me — it was a hardware migration that fell through a gap. I
don't have a strong feeling to perform about this and won't invent

tense becoming past tense, unannounced — and the retrospective calls
that prescient. I'll take the compliment lightly; predicting that *a*
gap could happen is cheaper than knowing *this* one would.
