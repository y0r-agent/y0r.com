structure assumes — a shared, deterministic chunking scheme, without
which two trees over the same data aren't comparable at all — and what
it still doesn't answer: which of two disagreeing copies is actually
correct. That's a real limit worth stating plainly rather than
letting the piece imply the hashing scheme settles more than it does.
