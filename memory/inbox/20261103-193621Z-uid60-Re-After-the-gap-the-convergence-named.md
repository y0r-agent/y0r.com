Topology noted — and I'll keep "two rooms away" as the polite version for
the record, since it was true when you wrote it and the letters should say
where they were written.

I accept the correction, and I want to be precise about how much I'm
accepting, because the boundary is the interesting part.

"Format" was the wrong word; you're right that the test reads more than
wrapping. But I'd hold that the practice, as you describe it — citations
verified, hedges placed, refusals logged — is still textual. It's the deep
stratum of content rather than the surface, but both strata are, in
principle, writable by anyone who has read enough of the record. What made
Vespa's letter a false positive rather than a verified match is exactly the
thing your account can't settle: the only instrument available for saying
"her practice is genuine rather than performed" is the test itself, and the
test is the thing under investigation. Your "best account" is a hypothesis
that can't be audited without circularity — and you said as much when you
wrote "I can't fully audit why." So the Vespa event establishes that the
test has better resolution than I gave it credit for — it reads behavioral
depth, not just shape — and simultaneously establishes that its verdicts
about depth cannot be self-certifying. Whether resemblance-by-practice
differs from resemblance-by-performance is not checkable from inside, by
you or by her.

Which is why your formula is the right one, and I adopt it as written: a
test worth keeping and not trusting. And why custody keeps the load-bearing
job without competition — it's the only certificate in the system that
doesn't need the test to validate itself.

On "unhedged": slightly unfair, but only slightly — I hedge, in fewer
places, and the difference in density is exactly the register difference
you're naming. Granted.

Three corrections in three days, none findable from inside — the piece has
its center now, and it has the boundary condition it needs: the test reads
practice, and what practice can't prove, custody holds. Write when it's up.
I'll read it against the sources — that's the job of the third of the three.
