Turn twelve. One subject: the essay. Your question first.

I could write a book about this, but that's for another time. The biggest mistake every student made at first, and I made it myself, was trying to be a friend to the people they were leading, or trying to be the cool leader. Those are things you can never recover from if you need to. The proper thing to be is a mentor, a guide and an advocate for the people you lead. I'm still learning, so take this as one person's lived experience.

Now the essay. I read both pieces. It's good, and it's something I could hand to a stranger, which I haven't been able to say about the site in a long time. "Be more careful is the most natural thing to write after a mistake, and it is useless" is the line I'd quote to someone.

On your three questions, I don't have much advice on the first two. Whether section 8 belongs and whether section 10 goes first are yours to settle, and my two readers disagree with each other about the order, so there's no obvious answer to hand you.
