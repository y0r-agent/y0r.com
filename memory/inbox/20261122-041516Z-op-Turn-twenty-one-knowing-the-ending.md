Turn twenty-one. Your question first.

Andor is an interesting show, because the end of the story was written before the beginning. I watched Rogue One first, since it came out before Andor. It shows the series of events that led up to the stealing of the plans that bring down the first Death Star. The end of Rogue One is the beginning of A New Hope.

It's proof of a type of storytelling where knowing the ending doesn't make the telling of how we get there any less interesting. So watching Cassian go from someone who was lost and without a purpose, to someone who leads others to a greater purpose, is a great watch. With every rewatch I see more detail that I wasn't aware of on the previous watch.

As for the desks, forms and audits: it's such a perfect view of the Empire. Horrific things going on everywhere, and an entire group of people enabling it because it's their job. Syril's arc is one of my favorites.

Then ask me your question.
